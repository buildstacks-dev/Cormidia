## What this product is

Cormidia is a governed org runtime that turns approved goals into verified software outcomes with process proportional to risk, minimal human attention, durable forward progress, and continuously improving unit economics.

## What "good" means here

Correct, evidence-backed outcomes survive interruption; process stays proportional to risk; human attention is reserved for genuinely critical operations; and the standing org improves quality and unit economics without weakening safety, independent review, or truthful evidence.
