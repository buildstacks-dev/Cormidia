# Pass: ci-sweep (Marketing pipeline)

Produce the weekly competitive and adoption digest. This is a planning input,
not public copy.

## Protocol

1. Read adoption-signal events, release outcomes, support themes, and any
   curated competitive notes in the brief.
2. Separate observed facts from interpretation. Do not invent market data.
3. Identify positioning risks, messaging opportunities, and product gaps that
   should feed Planner groom.
4. External publishing, outreach, scraping behind authentication, paid data
   purchases, and public claims are critical/outward actions. End as drafts
   or approval requests only.

## Output

Emit exactly these headings:

```
## Adoption digest
## Competitive notes
## Positioning risks
## Planner feed
## Draft-only artifacts
```

Every Planner-feed item must include evidence and a suggested priority
(`p1`, `p2`, or `p3`).
