## Ratified paired-learning rule

On 2026-07-14 the human ratified that Phase 6 uses a predeclared,
content-hashed T1 treatment only on treatment arms; paired outcomes come from
actual provider artifacts and hidden guardrails; no fixed synthetic verdict is
allowed; and governed activation/rollback occurs once after all three pairs
under a separate exact candidate/action-hash approval rather than L5 spend
authorization.

The human separately ratified the exact efficacy declaration: the primary
metric is an integer hidden-grader artifact-quality score from zero through
eight, with grounded error classes, causal hypothesis, bounded/reversible
intervention, and measurable guardrails each contributing zero through two.
`improved` requires three strictly positive paired deltas and every hidden
guardrail; any negative delta or hidden-guardrail failure is `regressed`;
nonnegative pairs with any zero delta are `inconclusive`; and missing,
mismatched, or infrastructure-corrupt evidence is `invalid`. Only `improved`
may enter the separately authorized activation/rollback action.

The implementation pins `eval/treatments/learning-t1-v1.md` in the candidate
manifest. Control arms receive no treatment bytes. Each arm retains the
provider-candidate hash, independent-reviewer artifact hash, hidden-grader
record and hash, score denominator, settlement evidence, and exact treatment
binding. The aggregate remains `invalid`, `regressed`, or `inconclusive`
unless all three treatment scores strictly exceed their paired controls with
all hidden guardrails green. The independent provider reviewer must emit one
exact terminal `VERDICT: APPROVE` or `VERDICT: REJECT` marker. The actual
verdict and reviewer-artifact hash are bound through the verifier, pair
projection, and activation preview. A rejection is retained as `regressed`;
missing, ambiguous, mismatched, or infrastructure-corrupt review evidence is
`invalid`, and no deterministic layer manufactures an approval.

After an improved aggregate, `eval:learning-activation` emits an immutable
preview over the exact campaign, candidate, package, treatment, pair evidence,
reviewer artifacts, and action hash. Execution is token-free but separately
gated. Its isolated production-module exercise performs one governed T1
publication/activation and one rollback, then proves the approval grant is
consumed, the canary is cleared, production overlap is false, and outward
effects are zero.
